Reflective decision coaching

Structured one-to-one sessions for people standing in front of a decision that will shape the next several years. Not advice. A method for seeing what you have already thought, and what you have been walking around.

Who this is for

You are deciding something that does not have a right answer.

A good fit

You have thought about it a lot and got nowhere

Circling is not the same as exploring. Most people who feel stuck have gone very deep on two or three considerations and have not touched a fourth. The work is finding the fourth.

A good fit

Everyone around you has an opinion

Advice from people who love you is still advice about their life. You need a structure that puts your own reasoning back at the centre.

A good fit

The decision is personal, not technical

Career changes, relocations, leaving or staying, starting something. Decisions where the trade-offs are real and nobody can compute the answer for you.

Not a fit

What this is not

This is not therapy, counselling or mental health treatment, and it is not a substitute for them. If what you are carrying is grief, trauma or a crisis, a licensed professional is the right person and I will say so.

The method

Built from published research, not from a certificate mill.

The structure below is the human version of the system I built and evaluated with more than 190 participants across three studies. It is documented in four papers, which you are welcome to read before deciding whether to trust it.

Step one

Map what is already there

You talk, I map. We end up with an honest picture of the ground you have covered across the practical, the emotional and the lived, without me evaluating any of it.

Step two

Find the thin places

I show you the map and name what is thin. Then I stop talking. A gap in the map is not a mistake, and only you know whether it is settled, avoided or genuinely irrelevant.

Step three

You choose where to go

You decide which thin place is worth opening. In the research, people moved toward what they had underexplored 74% of the time when they were given the choice. Nobody had to push them.

One thing the method deliberately does not do is tell you what to choose. My studies measure the quality of the process, not the outcome of the decision. I will not claim it makes your choices turn out better, because I have not measured that.

Questions

Before you ask

Are you a licensed therapist?

No. I am a researcher. This is decision coaching, which means structured thinking support, and it is not clinical care. If a conversation needs a clinician, I will say so and stop.

Why would I pay a person for something your software does?

The software is better at counting and worse at everything else. My own research found that the same observable pattern can mean nine different things, and the system cannot tell which. A person can ask.

Will you tell me what to do?

No. The entire design principle behind my research is surface, do not steer. It would be strange to abandon it in person.

What happens to what I tell you?

It stays between us. Nothing from a session enters my research, and research participation and coaching are kept entirely separate.
