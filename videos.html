Why the channel exists

I learned data science in Persian-language forums and English-language documentation at the same time, which meant translating twice for every concept. Most Persian material stops at the syntax. Most English material assumes a background that many students in Iran do not have access to. The channel sits in that gap. It teaches in whichever language makes the idea land, and it always ends with running code.

The channel runs alongside my research rather than out of it. What connects them is the same instinct: give people the tools to see something themselves rather than handing them the conclusion.
