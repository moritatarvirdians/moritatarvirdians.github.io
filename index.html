I study how people think through hard decisions.

My research builds conversational agents that help someone see their own thinking more clearly, without telling them what to choose. I also teach data science to a few thousand learners as Morita DataLand.

What I do

One question, three places I work on it

The thread through all of it is the same. People already hold the reasons behind their own choices. What they usually lack is a clear view of the shape of their own thinking. Research, teaching and coaching are three ways of handing that view back.

Recent papers

  • EMNLP Findings2026

    Why This and Not That? A Collaborative Reflection Approach for Understanding Thought Coverage in Decision Making Support Dialog

    Morita Tarvirdians, Hayley Hung, Catharine Oertel

    An agent can see that you have said little about one side of a decision. It cannot see why. Across 62 participants and 232 steering moments we find nine distinct reasons behind one and the same observable pattern, and show that people move toward what they have underexplored 74% of the time when they get to choose.

    arXiv PDF BibTeX

  • UMAP2026

    Reflecti-Mate: A Conversational Agent for Adaptive Decision-Making Support Through System 1 and System 2 Thinking

    Morita Tarvirdians, Senthil Chandrasegaran, Hayley Hung, Catholijn M. Jonker, Catharine Oertel

    A web-based agent that tracks what a person has covered across head, heart and gut, and redirects the conversation toward whichever is thinnest.

    arXiv TU Delft

  • Preprint2025

    Reflection Before Action: Designing a Framework for Quantifying Thought Patterns for Increased Self-awareness in Personal Decision Making

    Morita Tarvirdians, and co-authors

    The measurement framework the later work is built on. It turns an open reflection into two numbers, how broadly and how deeply a person has thought.
