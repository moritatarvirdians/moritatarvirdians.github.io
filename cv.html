Profile

Research profile

PhD researcher in the Interactive Intelligence group at TU Delft, working at the intersection of conversational AI, user modelling, and human decision support. My research builds computational frameworks that let conversational agents model, mirror, and scaffold how people reason through high-stakes life decisions — integrating cognitive, emotional, and intuitive considerations rather than rational analysis alone. I draw on NLP, adaptive dialogue management, and cognitive theories such as dual-process theory, evaluating my systems through user studies. I am seeking a postdoctoral position where I can extend this line of work toward multi-party decision contexts, supporting groups of people with differing perspectives in scaffolding their own reasoning while finding common ground with their decision partners.

Experience

Research experience

  1. 2022 – present
    PhD Researcher (a 5-year PhD programme) Interactive Intelligence Group, TU Delft, Delft, The Netherlands
    • Designed and evaluated ReflectiMate (Tarvirdians et al., UMAP ’26), an adaptive conversational agent supporting pre-decision reflection through dynamic exploration–exploitation balancing and evolving user modelling.
    • Designed and evaluated a meta-reflection interface within ReflectiMate to support hybrid human–AI reflection through model-derived structural awareness and user interpretation; resulting work published in Findings of EMNLP 2026; follow-up work under review.
    • Developed PROBE (arXiv 2025), a computational framework for assessing pre-decision reflection across breadth and depth dimensions, validated through inter-coder agreement studies.
    • Designed and conducted 3 user studies (N = 230 participants) using Prolific, including experimental design, participant recruitment, ethics procedures, and sensitive-data management.
    • Built privacy-preserving locally hosted LLM pipelines on secure infrastructure for sensitive decision-making contexts.
